Political debate: is electoral work the route to overcoming institutions of power
Or is it class agitation for public intimidation
Or is it worker organizing and collective bargaining
Or is it lobbying to tax the wealthy and fund public work
Or is it nonprofits that are funded with rich people's money
Or is it nonprofits funded with small donations
Or is it nonprofits funded with government grants
Or is it business that grows capital and serves people by fair market exchange
Or is it zero-budget flat self-organized sharing and connecting?
These are the BIG OPTIONS, right?

Maybe it's like therapy: there isn't a single correct modality, and the most important thing is that the participants must believe in it in order for it to work.
